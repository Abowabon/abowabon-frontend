Fix the "When" Trap — The Purest Doing Language

In manifestation, the Being state comes first. The external event follows as a reflection of that state. "When" makes you wait for the effect to produce its own cause — an infinite loop with no resolution.

There is also a deeper problem: every "when" statement is a subconscious vote cast for "not yet." The universe, which mirrors your inner state, receives "not yet" and reflects it. The "when" never comes because you keep confirming it is in the future.

You have noticed that simply saying the desired amount — or "I now receive this from infinite intelligence" — activates your frisson. But you're unsure whether the feeling of settled having has occurred and been transmitted. This is the most important question you have asked.
