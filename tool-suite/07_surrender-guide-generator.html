The difference between surrender and giving up

Giving up says: "I no longer believe this is possible." Surrender says: "I believe so completely that I no longer need to carry it." One comes from despair. The other comes from trust. The body knows the difference instantly, even if the mind can't always articulate it.

The moment you stop checking for evidence of its absence is often the moment evidence of its presence begins to arrive. This isn't mysticism — it's the mechanics of attention. You find what you look for.
