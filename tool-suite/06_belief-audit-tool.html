Why your beliefs feel like facts

A belief repeated enough times stops feeling like an opinion and starts feeling like a description of reality. Most limiting beliefs were formed from a handful of early experiences, then reinforced by selectively noticing evidence that fits and ignoring evidence that doesn't.

The fix isn't forcing yourself to believe the opposite overnight — that triggers resistance. It's finding a new belief specific and believable enough that your nervous system doesn't reject it on contact.
