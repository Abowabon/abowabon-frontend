What is the SAFE Method?

S — Safety First. Regulate your nervous system before anything else. A contracted, threat-mode body cannot attract what it fears.

A — Acknowledge & Accept. Hold the desire clearly while accepting the current reality without denial or resistance.

F — Find a Floor. Identify the minimum you need to feel safe. This removes the desperation that repels desire.

E — Engage & Release. Take one aligned daily action. Then release attachment to the outcome until tomorrow.
