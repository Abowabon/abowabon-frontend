Reality Transurfing and Tufti the Priestess — Every Concept Explained

Vadim Zeland's Reality Transurfing is one of the most sophisticated frameworks for intentional reality creation ever written. But the books are dense, translated from Russian, and not easy to apply. This guide breaks down every concept into plain English with practical daily applications.

This is the most comprehensive free English-language guide to Reality Transurfing currently available. It covers all 9 Transurfing concepts and all 8 Tufti concepts with examples and practices.

Concept 01
The Space of Variations

Zeland's foundational idea is that all possible versions of reality already exist simultaneously — an infinite field of variations, like an enormous library where every shelf holds a different version of your life. You do not create your future; you select it from this space by moving to the life track where it already exists.

This changes everything about how you approach manifestation. You are not trying to force something into existence from nothing. You are tuning your internal frequency to match the sector of the Space of Variations where what you want is already the case.

Key principle

Your thoughts, feelings, and beliefs act as a tuning dial — they determine which sector of the Space of Variations you navigate toward. Change the dial, and you move to a different track.

Daily practice — Space of Variations
1Each morning, before checking your phone, ask: "Which version of today am I selecting?" Choose consciously rather than letting the default version find you.
2When something goes wrong, say internally: "This is one variation. The variation I prefer also exists." Then feel — not think — what that preferred variation feels like.
3Once daily, sit quietly and imagine that the life you want already exists somewhere in the space. You are simply moving toward it, not building it from scratch.
Concept 03
Pendulums — the energy thieves

A Pendulum is any thought structure that feeds on human energy and attention — organisations, belief systems, ideologies, social trends, even arguments. Every time you engage with a Pendulum (worry about it, fight it, agree with it), you feed it energy and it pulls you further into its orbit.

Pendulums are not evil — they are simply systems that self-perpetuate through human attention. But they will divert you from your own life track if you let them. News cycles, social media outrage, workplace drama, financial fear narratives — these are all Pendulums competing for your energy.

The Pendulum trap

You cannot destroy a Pendulum by fighting it — fighting is feeding it. The only solution is to reduce its importance and withdraw your energy. Indifference, not resistance, is the protection.

Pendulum protection practice
1Identify the 3 Pendulums most active in your life right now (news, a workplace conflict, financial anxiety, social comparison). Name them.
2Practice the phrase: "This is a Pendulum. I withdraw my energy." Say it when you feel pulled. Do not fight — just quietly step aside.
3Redirect the freed energy immediately toward your own goal. Energy pulled from Pendulums becomes fuel for your own track.

Concept 04
Importance — the great repeller

In Transurfing, Importance is the single greatest obstacle to getting what you want. The more important something becomes to you — the more you need it, fear losing it, or obsess over it — the more the Balancing Forces of the universe work to prevent it from reaching you.

This is counterintuitive but observable: the things people want most desperately are often the things that elude them longest. The mechanism is not mystical. When you inflate the importance of something, you enter a state of tension and grasping that literally repels the relaxed, receptive state needed to attract it.

Reducing importance

"It would be wonderful to have this. I am also perfectly fine without it." That sentence, genuinely felt rather than forced, is the Transurfing prescription for importance reduction. Both clauses must be true simultaneously.

Importance reduction — daily exercise
1Identify your most inflated importance today. What are you gripping most tightly? Name it specifically.
2Write: "[X] would be wonderful. I am also genuinely fine without it right now." Make sure both parts are true. If the second part feels like a lie, you need to find what is genuinely true in it.
3Breathe, relax the body, and repeat twice. Notice if any physical tension releases.

Concept 08
The Mirror Principle

The world is a mirror. It reflects back not what you want but what you radiate. If you radiate confidence, you receive confirmation of your confidence. If you radiate lack, you receive confirmation of lack. The mirror never lies and it never flatters — it simply reflects.

This means that changing your outer circumstances begins with changing your inner radiation — not with action, though action follows naturally. The shift is from "I'll feel good when I have it" to "I radiate the frequency of already having it." The mirror then reflects that frequency back as outer circumstance.

The mirror delay

The mirror has a delay — your outer circumstances reflect an earlier version of your inner state. This is why people give up: they change their inner state and see no immediate outer change. But the mirror is always catching up. Consistency bridges the delay.

Mirror principle — morning practice
1Before leaving home: ask "What am I radiating right now?" Check body tension, thought quality, emotional state. The mirror will reflect exactly this.
2Consciously choose to radiate what you want reflected. Abundance, calm confidence, welcome and openness. Hold it for 60 seconds.
3At the end of each day: look for evidence the mirror responded. Even small reflections count — a kind interaction, an unexpected opportunity, a moment of ease.
Tufti the Priestess
Tufti — becoming the Director

Tufti the Priestess is Zeland's second major work — more advanced, more challenging, and in many ways more practical. Where Transurfing focuses on navigating the Space of Variations, Tufti introduces the concept of the Director: someone who does not merely observe their life as a film, but who consciously directs it.

The core Tufti principle is the Frame Film. Reality is being projected frame by frame, and there is a tiny gap between frames — a space where you can insert your intent. Most people are spectators, pulled along by the film. The Director inserts their will at the frame gap and steers the story.

Wide Gaze — the Director's tool

The Wide Gaze is a state of expanded, peripheral awareness where you observe reality without being absorbed into it. Like an actor who inhabits a role fully but never forgets they are acting. The Wide Gaze is what allows you to direct rather than be directed.

Wide Gaze practice — Tufti
1While walking or commuting: expand your visual field to include as much peripheral vision as possible, without moving your eyes. Hold this for 2 minutes.
2In this expanded state, silently intend the outcome of your next interaction or situation. Not anxiously — calmly, like a Director who knows how the scene ends.
3Notice how this state changes the interaction. Reality responds to directed consciousness differently than to unconscious drifting.
