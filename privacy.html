01 Who we are

Abowabon ("we", "us", "our") provides a free AI-powered manifestation app, web tools, and related digital content at abowabon.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what choices you have.

This policy applies to the Abowabon web app, the Mode Checker quiz, the Transurfing guide hub, the email capture page, and any associated mobile apps.

03 How AI guide generation works

When you type a desire and request a guide, that text is sent securely to our backend server, which forwards it to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate your personalised SAFE Method guide. We do not store the content of your desire on our servers beyond the time needed to process the request.

We recommend not entering highly sensitive personal information (full names of others, medical details, financial account numbers) into the desire field, even though the system is designed to handle general goals like "financial abundance" or "career success" safely.

04 Cookies & advertising (Google AdSense)

This site displays advertisements via Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet
  • You can disable cookies entirely in your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended
AdSense disclosure: This site is a participant in the Google AdSense programme. We earn revenue from ads displayed on the site, which does not affect the price or availability of any free content.

05 Email & Mailchimp

If you sign up for our free guide or newsletter, your name and email address are stored with Mailchimp, our email service provider. We use this to send you the requested content and occasional updates about Abowabon.

  • You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Mailchimp's own privacy practices apply to data they process on our behalf — see Mailchimp's Privacy Policy

06 Local storage on your device

Abowabon uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to save your streak, journal entries, scripting notebook, and progress data directly on your device. This data:

  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Is only accessible from the same browser and device where it was created
  • Can be exported at any time using the "Export my data" feature in your Profile
  • Can be permanently deleted using the "Reset all data" feature, or by clearing your browser's site data

07 Your rights (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

If you are located in the UK or EU, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your email and associated data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing of your data for marketing purposes
  • Withdraw consent — unsubscribe from emails at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.
