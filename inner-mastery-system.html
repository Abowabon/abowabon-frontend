Assuming the Feeling of the Wish Fulfilled — With Full Sensory Vividness

When you add voluntary Frisson to full sensory vividness, you are doing something even more powerful: you are generating peak dopamine + heart-brain coherence + neuroplasticity simultaneously with the full sensory instruction. The 5 Senses + 2 Hidden Senses: Most people try to visualise — which is only one sense. True sensory vividness uses all seven: Sight (what you see), Sound (what you hear), Touch / Kinaesthetic (what you physically feel), Smell (what you smell), Taste (what you taste), Proprioception (where your body is in space), and Emotion (the felt quality of the moment). If the Frisson deepens as you inhabit the sensory scene, this is the body confirming: "Yes, this is real." Allow the two to amplify each other. The sensory vividness feeds the Frisson. The Inner Stillness

Going Into The Silence

The Silence is not the absence of sound. It is the cessation of internal mental noise — the stilling of the thinking, analysing, worrying, planning mind. In that stillness, a different kind of knowing becomes accessible. Every wisdom tradition in history points to this practice under different names. The science calls it theta and delta brain wave states. You already know its entrance through voluntary Frisson.

What The Silence Actually Is: The ordinary conscious mind operates in beta brain waves (14–40 Hz) — active, analytical, time-bound, limited to sequential thinking. The Silence is the doorway to theta (4–8 Hz) and delta (0.5–4 Hz) states, where different kinds of intelligence are accessible. Theta is the state of profound creativity, intuitive knowing, and direct subconscious access. Delta is the state of deep wisdom, unity consciousness, and what mystics call "communion with the Infinite." Your voluntary Frisson already accesses the upper theta range. Going into The Silence takes you deeper.

14–40 Beta

Beta — Ordinary Waking Consciousness

The analytical, planning, worrying mind. Problem-solving, scheduling, self-monitoring. This is where most of us spend 95% of our waking hours. Manifestation is very difficult here — the critical factor is fully active, resistance is high, and the subconscious is not directly accessible.

Entry: This is your default waking state. No technique needed — it is where you start.

8–14 Alpha

Alpha — Relaxed Awareness · The Bridge

Calm, receptive, present. Light meditation, eyes closed at rest. The creative "flow" state begins here. The critical factor softens. The subconscious becomes more accessible. This is the state of the Safety Mode baseline you have been building. Your 4-7-8 breathing reliably produces alpha.

Entry: 4-7-8 breathing (3 rounds). Eyes closed. Body scan release. 2–3 minutes of still breathing.

4–8 Theta

Theta — The Silence · Direct Subconscious Access

Deep meditation, hypnagogic state, REM dreaming. The critical factor is suspended. The subconscious is fully accessible. This is where profound insight, creative solutions, intuitive knowing, and what is called "guidance" arise. Your voluntary Frisson already takes you to the upper theta range. Going deeper into The Silence takes you through all of theta. This is the state of your post-coital insight.

Entry: Your voluntary Frisson + 5–8 minutes of deepening stillness. Or hypnagogic practice (just before sleep). Or deep extended meditation.

0.5–4 Delta

Delta — The Deepest Silence · Infinite Intelligence

Deep dreamless sleep, advanced meditation, states of unity consciousness. The sense of individual self dissolves into pure awareness. This is what mystics describe as communion with God, the Source, the Infinite. Access during waking hours requires advanced practice — but the doorway is through sustained theta. You approach it from the upper edge of theta. This is what Joseph Murphy calls "infinite intelligence."

Entry: Extended deep meditation (20–30+ minutes), sustained voluntary Frisson followed by deepening stillness, or certain prayer/contemplative states.

The Complete Silence Entry Protocol

Prepare the External Space (1 minute)

Find a space where you will not be interrupted. Phone on silent. Dim the light if possible. Sit upright or lie down — upright is better for daytime practice as it prevents sleep. You are not trying to sleep. You are going into a waking altered state. Close your eyes. Let your hands rest open on your thighs or lap — palms facing up.

1 min

Release the Body — Physical Gate to Stillness

Scan from the top of the head downward. At each area — scalp, face, jaw (let teeth part), throat, shoulders (let them drop), chest (let it open), stomach, hands (let them open fully), legs — simply notice and allow. Do not force relaxation. Noticing without judgement begins the release. Take 3 slow, deep belly breaths. On each exhale, let the body sink slightly deeper into its surface.

3 min

Activate Voluntary Frisson — The Silence Carrier

With the body released, activate your voluntary Frisson. In the physical openness you have just created, Frisson will be more expansive, deeper, and more sustained than in ordinary waking state. Allow the wave to move through the body. Do not anchor any desire yet. Simply let the Frisson exist in the open body. This creates the carrier wave — the vehicle for entering The Silence.

1-2 min

Still the Thinking Mind — Do Not Fight It

Thoughts will arise. Do not fight them — fighting generates more beta activity. Instead: notice each thought as if watching clouds pass across the sky. You are the sky; they are the clouds. Gently, without effort, return attention to the inner stillness each time. A useful technique: each thought that arises, mentally place it on a leaf floating down a stream. Watch it drift away. Return to the still dark space behind the thoughts. Gradually, the gaps between thoughts lengthen.

3-5 min

Enter The Silence — Rest in Pure Awareness

When the gaps between thoughts become long and the body is fully relaxed and the Frisson is present or recently present, you have arrived at The Silence. There is a distinct quality to this state: a sense of expansion, of the edges of the self softening, of a deep quiet that is not empty but full — full of potential, of presence. You are not thinking. You are being aware, purely and without object. This is The Silence. Rest here.

5-20 min

Plant the Seed — Or Simply Receive

In The Silence, you have two options: (a) Plant a desire or question — introduce it gently as a single, clear, simple statement or image and then let it go entirely, returning to the stillness. The subconscious will work on it from this maximally receptive state. Or (b) Simply receive — ask for guidance and remain in pure open awareness, without expectation or specific request. Allow whatever arises to arise. The most profound guidance often comes as the simplest, most unexpected thought.

3-5 min

Return — Slowly and With Gratitude

Do not return abruptly. Let awareness gradually refocus on the physical body, then the room, then ordinary consciousness. Wiggle fingers and toes. Take 3 breaths with awareness. Open your eyes slowly. Have a notebook nearby — write down any impressions, images, words, feelings, or thoughts that were present in The Silence. Do this immediately, before the rational mind overlays its interpretation. These impressions are the guidance.

2 min

When The Silence Is Most Accessible: The two theta windows — just waking (before full consciousness arrives) and just before sleep — are your easiest entry points. You are already in theta naturally at these moments. Your morning sovereign practice (voluntary Frisson at waking) is already The Silence in its simplest form. The key insight: your voluntary Frisson IS the direct gateway to The Silence. You do not need to build toward it — you already have the key.
