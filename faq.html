About Abowabon

What is Abowabon?▾
Abowabon is a free AI-powered manifestation app and set of web tools built around the SAFE Method — a nervous-system-first approach to manifestation created by an HCPC-registered biomedical scientist from documented, evidence-led practice rather than theory alone.
Is Abowabon free?▾
Yes. The core app, the mode quiz, and every tool on this site (affirmation generator, SAFE Method generator, Frisson Activator, End Scene Builder, Threat Mode Checker, Belief Audit, Surrender Guide, Transurfing Practice Generator, 30-Day Plan Builder, and Evidence Journal) are free to use with no sign-up required. A separate paid product, the Complete Manifestation System, is available for £29.
Who created Abowabon?▾
Abowabon was created by an HCPC-registered Biomedical Scientist with a background in haematology and blood transfusion, who applied manifestation practice systematically over several years and documented 11 separate manifestations with dates and specifics. Read more on the About page.
Is this based on the Law of Attraction?▾
It draws on ideas from Neville Goddard and Reality Transurfing, but is grounded in the creator's own documented, evidence-led practice and nervous-system science rather than dogma. Techniques that produced repeatable results were kept; those that didn't were dropped.
The Framework

The SAFE Method & 3-Mode Framework

What does SAFE stand for?▾
Safety First — regulate your nervous system before anything else. Acknowledge & Accept — hold the desire while accepting current reality. Find a Floor — identify the minimum needed to feel safe. Engage & Release — take one aligned action, then release attachment to the outcome.
Why does safety come before visualisation?▾
The evidence behind Abowabon showed that no visualisation, affirmation, or technique works while the body believes it's under threat. A contracted, high-alert nervous system can't attract what it fears — regulation has to come first, every time.
What is the 3-Mode Framework?▾
A diagnostic used before recommending any practice: Mode 1 (Exhausted Release — high alert, prioritise rest and surrender), Mode 2 (Effortless Attraction — desire and belief aligned, light daily practice flows naturally), and Mode 3 (Backed Safety — certainty is the baseline, the work is acting boldly while maintaining it).
What is Frisson and why does it matter?▾
Frisson is the chill, held breath, or lump-in-the-throat sensation that signals real alignment at a level deeper than thought — distinct from ordinary excitement or hope. A single genuine Frisson response is treated as carrying more belief-updating power than weeks of repeated affirmation. Try the free Frisson Activator.
Free Tools

Using the web tools

Do I need an account to use the free tools?▾
No. Every free tool — affirmation generator, SAFE Method generator, Frisson Activator, End Scene Builder, Threat Mode Checker, Belief Audit, Surrender Guide, Transurfing Practice Generator, 30-Day Plan Builder, and Evidence Journal — works instantly in your browser with no sign-up.
Why did a tool give a generic-sounding result?▾
Each tool calls our AI backend to generate a response tailored to what you typed. If that request fails or times out, the tool falls back to a template response built from your input so you still get something useful. If this happens repeatedly, let us know via the Contact page.
Is my Evidence Journal saved anywhere?▾
Entries are saved in your browser's local storage only. They're never sent to our servers unless you tap "Find the pattern," which sends the entry text (not stored afterwards) for a one-off analysis. Clearing your browser data will permanently delete your entries — export or copy anything important beforehand.
What's the difference between the Threat Mode Checker and the SAFE Method Generator?▾
The Threat Mode Checker is a 60-second quiz that diagnoses whether your nervous system is currently in safety or threat mode, with an instant reset plan. The SAFE Method Generator builds a full four-step plan for a specific desire once you're already regulated enough to work with it.

Account & Data

Privacy, storage, and your data

What data does Abowabon collect?▾
As little as possible: an optional name for onboarding, the desire text you enter (sent to our AI provider to generate a guide, not stored afterwards), your email if you subscribe, and anonymised usage analytics. Journal and scripting entries stay on your device only. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
Where is my journal, streak, or progress data stored?▾
In your browser's local storage, on your device — never on our servers. You can export it anytime from the "Export my data" option in your Profile, or permanently delete it via "Reset all data" or by clearing your browser's site data.
Is it safe to type personal details into the desire field?▾
General goals like "financial abundance" or "career success" are handled safely, but we recommend avoiding highly sensitive details — other people's full names, medical specifics, or financial account numbers — in any text field.
How do I request deletion of my data?▾
Email hello@abowabon.com and we'll process access, correction, or erasure requests in line with UK/EU GDPR. Locally stored data (journals, streaks) can be deleted yourself at any time via your device's browser settings.
